Your whole day, in one little box

Boxlet is a visual micro-journal. Every day gets one board — drop in a photo, a note, a checklist, your mood, what you spent. Five seconds a moment, and a month you can actually look back on.

FreeNo account needediOS & Android

Made for people who quit journaling

Most diaries die on day three, in front of a blank page. Boxlet has no page — only cards, and each one takes seconds.

Five seconds a moment

Pick a card type, tap, done. If you can take a photo or tick a box, you can keep a journal.

One app instead of four

Notes, to-dos, spending and photos live on the same board, so the day is in one place — and so is the month.

A few friends, not the internet

Share a day with the people you'd actually tell. They can react and comment. No followers, no algorithm — and expenses are never shared.

Ten kinds of paper

Every card type has its own faint tint. Up close each one reads like paper; from a distance a full board looks like a small collection of it.
